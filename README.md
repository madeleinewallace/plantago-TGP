**Title**: Transgenerational plasticity in response to water limitation leads to reductions in fitness in _Plantago patagonica_, an arid-adapted annual plant

**Authors**: Madeleine Wallace, Rachel M. Mitchell

**Corresponding author**: Madeleine Wallace, maddiewallace@arizona.edu

**Status**: in prep


Our study examines how within-generation and transgenerational plasticity shape trait responses to water limitation across eleven climatically distinct populations of _Plantago patagonica_, a seed banking desert annual. Specifically, we assessed phenotypic differences in several performance and fitness traits to determine 1) if there was evidence of transgenerational plasticity in response to water limitation, 2) if collection site climate variability affected the magnitude of the transgenerational response, and 3) if the response was adaptive.
